Stop order: who gets the guest first?

Every press tour in the archive — a guest appearing on two or more different hosts' shows within 30 days of each other — laid out as a route. Columns are the 1st, 2nd, 3rd… stop of the tour; each band is a set of guests that went from one host to the next, as thick as the number of tours that took that leg. Hosts are sorted from habitual openers (top) to habitual closers (bottom). The small grey numbers between columns are the median days between stops. Hover a band or block for details; the second chart tallies how often each host opens or closes a tour.

The route

Blocks are hosts at a given stop; bands connect consecutive stops of the same tour.

Opener or closer?

How many tours each host opened (left, navy) versus closed (right, red). Middle stops are rare and not drawn.
