The Circuit

The 40 most-booked guests across the 17 shows, one row each, one dot per appearance placed by date and colored by host. A red bar under a run of dots marks a press tour: the same guest hitting two or more different hosts' shows within 30 days. Long, many-colored rows are the perennials everyone books; a row that collapses into one red bar is a one-time tour. Re-sort to compare who is booked most, who ranges widest across hosts, and who is hot right now. Hover a dot for the episode, or a host in the legend to isolate their bookings.

Guest names are pulled from feed text by regex, so hosts appearing as guests on each other's shows (italic navy) are real, but a few rows may merge or split people with similar names. Feed-label artifacts like "Lots More" and "Model Talk" are filtered out by hand.
