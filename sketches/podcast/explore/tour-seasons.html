Tour seasons

Every month since 2015, how many episodes across these shows were press-tour stops — a guest hitting two or more different hosts within 30 days — stacked by which host booked them. Bar height is tour episodes that month; the labels above each year count tours that started that year. The lower line is a 12-month rolling share: of all episodes with a named guest, what fraction were tour stops. A rising line means the circuit is synchronizing — the same people are being booked by the same hosts in the same weeks. Hover a month for the tours running; hover a host in the legend to isolate them.

A tour is the same guest (names folded for diacritics, periods, middle initials) on 2+ hosts' shows with ≤30 days between consecutive appearances. Guest names are regex-extracted from feed text, so counts are a lower bound. The number of hosts publishing grows over time (Hard Fork and Dwarkesh start in 2022), which inflates the raw bars; the share line partly corrects for volume but not for the extra pairing opportunities.
