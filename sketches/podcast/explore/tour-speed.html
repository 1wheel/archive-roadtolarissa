How fast is a podcast press tour?

Every guest who appeared on two or more different hosts' shows within 30 days of each other is a "tour": 66 of them across ~7,300 episodes. Each dot is one tour, placed by how many days elapsed between its first and last stop, and stacked into rows by how many distinct hosts the guest hit ( 2 hosts, 3+). The vertical tick in each row is the median. Below, the gray bars count the individual hops between consecutive stops. Hover a dot to see the itinerary.
