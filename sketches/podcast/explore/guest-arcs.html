Who shares guests with whom

Each dot is a host; an arc joins two hosts every time the same guest appeared on both of their shows within 30 days of each other (a press tour), and its thickness is how often that happened, 2014–2026. The hosts are ordered so the busiest corridors sit side by side — the Ezra Klein ↔ Terry Gross and Derek Thompson ↔ Terry Gross lanes carry most of the traffic. Hosts labeled in red almost never share a guest with anyone; Ira Glass never does. Hover an arc for the guests and dates, or a host for their partners.
