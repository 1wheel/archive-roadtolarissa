The Podcast Tour Detector

Every episode of the shows hosted by Ezra Klein, Derek Thompson, Joe Weisenthal & Tracy Alloway, and Nate Silver — one dot per episode, 2015–2026. A guest who turns up on two or more of these hosts' shows inside a short window is on a press tour and gets a bold dot: two hosts, three or more. Inspired by Jasmine Sun's August 2026 data-center blitz.

tour window: 30 days

Sources: public RSS feeds, Apple's podcast catalog for the paywalled Ezra Klein Show back catalog, and a union of Wayback Machine feed snapshots for 538 Politics (which only covers Nov 2018 on). Guests are pulled out of episode titles and descriptions with regexes, so a few are missed or mangled. Risky Business ended Jan 2026 — Nate did his Jasmine Sun interview in print. Data: episodes.json
