The Podcast Tour Detector

Every episode of the shows hosted by Ezra Klein, Derek Thompson, Joe Weisenthal & Tracy Alloway, Nate Silver, PJ Vogt, Kevin Roose & Casey Newton, Nilay Patel, Tyler Cowen, and Dwarkesh Patel — one dot per episode, 2014–2026. A guest who turns up on two or more of these hosts' shows inside a short window is on a press tour and gets a bold dot: two hosts, three or more. Hover or search a guest to see every appearance they've ever made. Inspired by Jasmine Sun's August 2026 data-center blitz.

tour window: 30 days

Sources: public RSS feeds, Apple's podcast catalog for the paywalled Ezra Klein Show back catalog, and a union of Wayback Machine feed snapshots for 538 Politics (which only covers Nov 2018 on). Guests are pulled out of episode titles and descriptions with regexes plus a Claude Haiku sweep of the leftovers, so a few are still missed or mangled. Click a dot to open the episode.
